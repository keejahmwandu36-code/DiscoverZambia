Natural Wonder

Livingstone, Southern Province

Victoria Falls

Experience one of the world's most celebrated waterfalls, formed where the Zambezi River plunges into a dramatic gorge.

Best known for: Waterfall viewpoints, the rainforest, scenic photography and adventure activities.

Visitor tip: Water levels and visibility vary by season. Check local conditions before travelling.

Wildlife

Eastern Province

South Luangwa National Park

Explore a celebrated wildlife destination known for safari experiences, diverse habitats and the Luangwa River ecosystem.

Best known for: Guided walking safaris, game drives and opportunities to observe wildlife in its natural habitat.

Visitor tip: Safari conditions vary seasonally. Arrange visits with established local operators.

Safari & Nature

Along the Zambezi River

Lower Zambezi National Park

Discover river scenery, wildlife and outdoor adventures in a remarkable national park along the Zambezi.

Best known for: Wildlife viewing, river-based activities and scenic safari experiences.

Visitor tip: Use licensed guides and follow wildlife safety instructions.

National Park

Western and Central Zambia

Kafue National Park

Explore one of Zambia's largest national parks, with varied habitats, wildlife and expansive natural landscapes.

Best known for: Extensive wilderness, wildlife viewing, birdlife and varied ecosystems.

Visitor tip: Distances can be substantial, so plan transport and accommodation before travelling.

Lake & Leisure

Southern Province

Lake Kariba

Enjoy expansive waters, peaceful scenery and lakeside experiences around Siavonga and other accessible areas.

Best known for: Boating, fishing, scenic views and lakeside holidays.

Visitor tip: Follow boating safety rules and check lake conditions before going on the water.

History

Northern Province

Shiwa Ng'andu

Discover a historic estate with distinctive architecture and a story connected to Zambia's twentieth-century history.

Best known for: Historic buildings, the estate's heritage and its distinctive setting in northern Zambia.

Visitor tip: Confirm access arrangements and visiting conditions before making the journey.
